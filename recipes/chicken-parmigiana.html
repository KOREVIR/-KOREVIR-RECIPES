Chicken Parmigiana

A beloved Australian pub classic featuring crispy chicken breast, rich tomato sauce and golden melted mozzarella cheese.

Prep
20 mins
Cook
30 mins
Serves
4 People

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • ½ cup Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cups tomato passata
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • Salt & Pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. Slice chicken breasts in half.
  3. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Coat in egg then breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan.
  5. Pan fry until golden.
  6. Transfer to a baking tray.
  7. Cover with tomato sauce.
  8. Add mozzarella cheese.
  9. Bake for 20 minutes.
  10. Serve with chips and salad.

Nutrition

  • Calories: 620 kcal
  • Protein: 48 g
  • Carbohydrates: 25 g
  • Fat: 31 g
