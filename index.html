Care Guide

Caring for Your KOREVIR Spoons

Premium Teak wood requires simple, mindful care to maintain its beauty for generations.

01

Wash

Hand wash your KOREVIR spoons with mild dish soap and warm water. Avoid abrasive sponges or harsh detergents that can strip the wood's natural oils.

02

Dry

Dry immediately with a soft, clean towel. Never leave your spoons wet or soaking in water, as prolonged moisture exposure can cause the wood to swell, crack, or develop stains.

03

Oil

When the wood appears dry or dull, apply a thin, even layer of food-grade mineral oil. Allow the oil to penetrate, then wipe away any excess. Regular oiling keeps the wood nourished.

04

Preserve

Store your KOREVIR spoons upright in a utensil holder or lay them flat in a cool, dry drawer. Avoid direct sunlight and heat sources to preserve their natural beauty.

05

Savor

With proper care, your KOREVIR spoons will last a lifetime, becoming more beautiful with each use. The natural oils in teak deepen with age, creating a unique patina.

Why Teak

The Natural Choice

Teak is more than just wood — it's a material that embodies strength, beauty, and timeless elegance.

Natural Beauty

Teak is prized for its warm, golden-brown tones that deepen and enrich with age, developing a unique patina that only improves over time.

Sustainably Sourced

Our teak is harvested from sustainable plantations, ensuring minimal environmental impact. Each spoon is crafted with respect for nature.

Gentle on Cookware

Teak is surprisingly gentle on your most delicate cookware. It won't scratch non-stick coatings, enamel, or stainless steel surfaces.

Made to Last

Teak is one of the most durable hardwoods in the world. With proper care, KOREVIR spoons will remain in excellent condition for decades.
