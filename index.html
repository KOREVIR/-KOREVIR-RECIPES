Care

Caring for Your KOREVIR Teak Spoons

Your KOREVIR wooden spoons are made from premium Teak wood – durable, beautiful, and built to last.

Why Teak

Naturally rich in oils, resistant to water and warping.

First Use

Wash, dry, apply mineral oil, and let absorb overnight.

Daily Cleaning

Hand wash with mild soap. Never soak. Dry immediately.

Oiling

Apply food-grade mineral oil when dry. Wipe excess.

Storage

Keep in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.

Avoid

Never dishwasher, microwave, or soak in water.
