Care Guide

Caring for Your KOREVIR Spoons

Premium Teak wood requires simple, mindful care to maintain its beauty for generations.

Wash & Dry

Hand wash with mild soap, dry immediately.

Oil

Apply mineral oil when dry.

Store

Keep in cool, dry place.

Avoid

Never dishwasher or soak.

Why Teak

The Natural Choice

Teak is one of the most durable and beautiful woods in the world. Its natural oils protect against water, stains, and warping, making it the ideal material for kitchen utensils.

Every KOREVIR spoon is crafted from sustainably sourced Teak, ensuring both quality and environmental responsibility.

  • Naturally water-resistant
  • Rich, warm color
  • Long-lasting durability
  • Sustainable sourcing
