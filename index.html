Product Care

Caring for Your KOREVIR Teak Spoons

Your KOREVIR wooden spoons are made from premium Teak wood – durable, beautiful, and built to last. Follow these simple steps to keep them in perfect condition for years to come.

Why Teak?

Teak is naturally rich in oils, making it resistant to water, stains, and warping. It's one of the hardest woods, perfect for daily cooking.

First Use

Wash with warm, soapy water, rinse, and dry immediately. Apply a thin layer of food-grade mineral oil or coconut oil. Let it absorb overnight.

Daily Cleaning

Hand wash with mild dish soap and warm water. Rinse well. Never soak in water. Dry immediately with a soft towel.

Oiling

Apply food-grade mineral oil or walnut oil every 4-6 weeks, or when the wood looks dry. Rub in, let absorb, then wipe off excess.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat sources. Keep them upright in a utensil holder or lay them flat.

Avoid

Never put your KOREVIR spoons in the dishwasher, microwave, or leave them soaking in water. Extreme heat and moisture cause cracking.
