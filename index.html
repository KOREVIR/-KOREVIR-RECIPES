Care Guide

Caring for Your KOREVIR Spoons

Premium Teak wood requires simple, mindful care to maintain its beauty for generations.

01

Wash

Hand wash with mild dish soap and warm water.

02

Dry

Dry immediately with a soft, clean towel.

03

Oil

Apply food-grade mineral oil when the wood looks dry.

04

Store

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

05

Enjoy

With proper care, your KOREVIR spoons will last a lifetime.
